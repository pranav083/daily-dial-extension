# A look inside

## Painting a day

Pick a category beside the dial, then drag around the ring. A full day takes
about ten seconds. The panel on the right updates as you go.

![A painted day, with the score, breakdown and streak](getting-started/02-day-painted.png)

## The first thing you see

Three cards, then you are painting. Everything except painting is optional.

![The welcome screen](getting-started/01-onboarding.png)

## Categories are yours

Ten slots — rename them, recolour them, change whether each counts for or
against your score, hide the ones you do not use. Days store the slot, never
the name, so renaming never rewrites your history, and never repaints a day
you logged months ago.

Six are on to begin with. Four more — Exercise, Sleep, Social, Errands — are
there and switched off, for the life areas the app's own observations most
often point at.

Each category's swatch is its colour picker. Change one and the ring, the
pens, the week strip, the bars, the calendar and the shared image all follow;
the ↺ beside it hands the slot back to the default.

![The category editor, with aliases](getting-started/03-settings-categories.png)

## A run you can actually keep

A challenge is a named stretch of consecutive days with a goal you set —
anything logged at all, so many minutes of one category, or a daily score.

The card says how many days in a row you have kept, what the goal is, whether
the day you are looking at counts, and whether the run can still be finished.
One missed day ends it and it tells you which day: a run that tolerates gaps
is a weaker thing wearing the same name. When it breaks, one button starts the
same challenge again from today.

## Your data, and getting it out

Export a spreadsheet or a full backup at any time. Google Drive backup is
optional and off until you switch it on.
