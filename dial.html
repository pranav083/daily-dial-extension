      The log

      Nothing written down in this range yet. Notes and intentions from the Day view show up here.

      Settings

      + counts toward your score, · is neutral, – counts against it. The circle button hides a category you don't use. The small field under each one is for aliases — your own words for the same category (e.g. "leetcode" or "mock interview" for Applications) — so the typed-entry box recognizes how you actually talk about your day, while it still paints in that category's one weighted colour.

      Daily reminders

      Notifications only fire while Chrome is running. The evening one tells you how much of the day is still unlogged.

      Weekly recap

      Off by default. A short summary of the week just gone — tracked time, top category, best day, streak.

      Worth noticing

      Six things, checked over the last few weeks — intentions you set but don't finish, stretches with no rest logged, your most productive hour going unprotected, and so on. Each states a fact; suggestions only appear if you ask for them. Off means the section never appears at all.

      Daily targets, per category

      Leave blank for no goal. Progress shows in the side panel once a target is set.

      Weekly targets, per category

      Independent of the daily targets above — set either, both, or neither per category.

      Challenge

      A day counter shown next to your streak. Unlike the streak it doesn't break — it just counts from the start date. Leave the name blank for none.

      Templates

      Templates carry painted time only — never notes, intentions, things to avoid, or your reflection. Applying one to a day that already has painted time asks for confirmation first, and it's undoable (⌘Z) afterward.

      Import

      JSON backups keep everything (days, categories, settings). CSV files only restore blocks of time, matched against your current categories by name. Merge adds missing days and leaves existing ones untouched; Replace wipes your history and restores exactly what's in the file.

      Fill multiple days

      Fills the chosen category (or erases) across every day from the start date to the end date, inclusive. Leave the time fields blank to fill the whole day. Capped at days per fill; each affected day is saved and undoable on its own, one at a time, from that day's view.

      Google Drive backup

      Roughly daily, quietly, to the same file. It only ever updates a backup you've already made by hand — it can't sign you in, so it does nothing until you've connected Drive at least once.

      Not backed up to Google Drive yet.

      Optional, and off unless you use it. Uses Google's appDataFolder — a storage space private to this extension that doesn't show up in your regular Drive and that no other app can read. This is the only feature in Daily Dial that sends data anywhere; everything else stays on this device. See Privacy for details.
